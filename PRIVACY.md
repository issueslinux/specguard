# SpecGuard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

SpecGuard is a read-only Atlassian Forge Jira issue panel operated by Daniel Paluszek (Linux Issues). For privacy enquiries, contact **issueslinux@gmail.com**.

## Data accessed

With the current user's Jira authorization, SpecGuard retrieves the issue key, summary and description from Jira to identify written acceptance criteria and potential evidence references. It does not require Jira write permissions or permission to access unrelated issues. Users can restrict access by changing their Jira permissions or uninstalling the app.

## Processing and retention

The app processes issue content in memory within the Atlassian Forge runtime and returns an analysis to the requesting user's panel. The current app implementation does **not** intentionally persist issue text to a separate database, send it to external AI/model providers, or sell it to third parties. Standard Atlassian Forge hosting, security logs, and service processing are governed by Atlassian's own terms and privacy documentation. This policy does not override those terms.

## Results

All acceptance criteria are marked **NOT VERIFIED**. SpecGuard does not verify tests, block changes or make automated decisions with legal or similar significant effects.

## Contact and changes

For a data question or deletion request related to the app, email **issueslinux@gmail.com**. Jira issue data remains managed by the Jira site owner; uninstalling the app stops its access, but does not remove Jira issues. Policy revisions will be dated in this repository.
